Your list stays yours

Nigmar! (נגמר!) has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot read your lists.

Effective November 17, 2026 · Applies to Nigmar! for iPhone

What is stored on your iPhone

Your lists, shopping history, the amounts you write down, the receipt photos you attach and the sections you choose are kept in the app’s own storage on your iPhone.

Sync and backup in iCloud

So that several people can see the same list, and so that it comes back after reinstalling, the app keeps a copy of it in Apple’s iCloud. Before the copy leaves the device it is encrypted with a key derived from your list code.

  • The developer can see that encrypted records exist, but does not have the code and so cannot read what is in them.
  • Anyone who has the list code or its invitation can see and change the list. Send them only to people who should be on it.
  • The list code is also kept in your iCloud Keychain and iCloud key-value storage, so that the list returns after a reinstall.

Sync requires iCloud to be signed in on the device and is also covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Adding by voice

When you add by voice, the app uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition to turn what was said into text. Recognition runs on the device where the device supports it, and otherwise on Apple’s servers. The app does not keep the recording.

Smart recognition with AI (optional)

This feature is off until you enter your own API key in the settings, for Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude. While it is on, the text of what you said or typed, together with the product names you have already used, is sent to the provider you chose, through your own account and key with that provider.

  • That information is covered by the provider’s own terms and privacy policy. On Google’s free tier, for example, Google may use the content to improve its products.
  • The key is stored in the device Keychain and is never sent to the developer.
  • Removing the key in the settings turns the feature off.

Camera and photos

The camera and the photo library are used only to attach a photo of a receipt to a shopping trip, when you choose to. The photo is kept on the device, and an encrypted copy is kept in iCloud together with the list.

Notifications

The reminder to write down what a trip cost is created on the device itself. The app does not send notifications from a server.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share usage data with developers, Apple may show the developer aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics.

Deleting your information

Any product, trip, receipt or list can be deleted in the app, and the deletion is applied to the copy in iCloud and to the other devices on the list. Deleting the app deletes what is stored on the device. For anything else, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
