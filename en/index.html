The grocery list for your home

Nigmar! (נגמר!, Hebrew for “we ran out!”) is a grocery list app in Hebrew. Add what ran out in a second, by typing or by voice. The list is sorted by supermarket section, and everyone at home sees and updates the same list.

Email support

We usually answer within two working days.

How do I add a product?

The app opens on the add screen with the keyboard up. Start typing, the field completes the word, and return adds it. You can write an amount before the name, for example “2 חלב”.

How do I add by voice?

Tap the microphone in the add field and say everything that ran out, at your own pace. When you are done, tap the button and all the products appear on one screen. Remove what is wrong and confirm the rest with one tap.

What is “smart recognition with AI”?

An optional feature that understands free speech, amounts and brand names. It works with your own API key, for Google Gemini (which has a free tier) or Anthropic Claude, entered in the settings. Without a key the app recognizes products with its built-in dictionary.

How do I share the list?

In the settings (the button with two people) tap “שליחת הזמנה” (send invitation). Whoever opens the invitation on an iPhone that has the app installed joins the list. If the link does not open, copy the message and paste it in the app under “קיבלתי הזמנה” (I received an invitation). Up to 8 accounts can share one list.

The list is not syncing

Sync works through iCloud. Check that the device is signed in to iCloud, that it is online, and that all devices use the same list code. The dot next to the settings button is green when the list is in sync.

I deleted the app. Is my list gone?

No. The list is kept encrypted in iCloud and comes back after reinstalling with the same iCloud account.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. The list is encrypted before it goes to iCloud. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app’s interface is in Hebrew.
